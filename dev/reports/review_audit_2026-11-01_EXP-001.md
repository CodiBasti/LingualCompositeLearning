### 1. Summary of Contribution

The authors propose a two-stage inference paradigm (*Lingual Composite Learning* / *Latent Semantic Bottleneck*), in which a foundation language model (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct`) classifies incomplete human commands in the presence of environmental context into discrete synthetic operators (`fufu`, `weli`, `zubu`, `kuku`), subsequently generating a motor plan bound to formal behavioral invariants.

In a pilot benchmark of 10 handcrafted scenarios with goal conflicts (e.g. urgency vs. crawling infant), the authors compare this approach (Condition C) against a naive unprompted baseline (Condition A) and an extensive Chain-of-Thought system prompt baseline (Condition B). Mean invariant recall increased from 27.5% (CoT) and 30.8% (Naive) to 50.8% (Concept Routing), alongside an 80.0% zero-shot concept classification accuracy.

---

### 2. Paper Strengths (Publication Assets)

1. **Real Model, Real Hardware, Zero Simulation Mocks:** The experiment was executed locally on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 using `Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` in bfloat16 at `temperature=0.0`. All raw logs are preserved as complete JSON artifacts.
2. **Empirical Confirmation of "Verbose CoT Breakdown":** The observation that verbose natural language explanations can degrade action fidelity in 14B models (27.5% vs. 30.8%) is a high-value empirical finding supporting the core thesis of *Attention Drift*.
3. **Decisive Gains in High-Stakes Scenarios:** In extreme goal-conflict scenarios (SCEN-003 crystal vase: 100% vs. 33.3%; SCEN-002 swing door: 66.7% vs. 33.3%; SCEN-004 executive meeting: 50.0% vs. 0.0%), invariant binding demonstrated clear superiority.
4. **Natural Human Interface:** The design preserves the requirement that human operators communicate in standard colloquial language without memorizing artificial tokens.

---

### 3. Critical Methodological Weaknesses Identified

#### 🔴 Limitation 1: $N = 10$ is a Feasibility Pilot, Not Statistical Proof
Ten scenarios validate the pipeline mechanics, but constitute grounds for desk rejection at NeurIPS/ICLR. A scaled dataset ($N \ge 100$) with rigorous confidence intervals and paired t-tests is strictly required. *(Resolved via VHC-100 and EXP-002/003).*

#### 🔴 Limitation 2: Two-Stage Prompt Routing vs. Latent Continuous Embeddings
Reviewers will question whether two-stage routing represents genuine representation learning or sequential prompt chaining. Continuous soft-token tuning via LoRA is required for end-to-end integration.

#### 🔴 Limitation 3: Rigid Keyword Evaluation Artifacts
In SCEN-005 and SCEN-009, all conditions scored 0% due to brittle regex matching, despite sensible actions generated by Condition C. A hybrid evaluation framework incorporating LLM-as-a-Judge is necessary. *(Resolved via `audit_judge.py`).*
